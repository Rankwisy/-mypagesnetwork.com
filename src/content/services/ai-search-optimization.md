## Search is moving into the answer

For a growing share of queries, buyers no longer scan ten blue links. Google shows an AI Overview above the results. ChatGPT and Perplexity answer "which accounting software for a small agency" with a shortlist and a few cited sources. If your brand is not in that answer, you are not in the consideration set, however well you rank below it.

These systems do not pick brands at random. They pull from pages that state things clearly, from sources that are consistent with each other, and from third-party sites they already trust: reviews, comparisons, industry directories, forums.

## How we improve AI visibility

We measure first. A fixed set of prompts, the questions your buyers actually ask, is run across the AI platforms that matter in your market. We record which brands are mentioned, which pages are cited, and where your competitors appear and you do not. That baseline is re-run every month.

Then we work on two fronts. On your own site, pages are restructured so they are easy to quote: clear definitions, specific numbers, comparison tables, honest pros and cons, and structured data. That editorial work runs through [content strategy](/services/content-strategy/). Off-site, we target the sources AI systems already cite in your niche, which often overlaps with [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) for businesses with physical locations.

Traditional rankings still matter, since AI Overviews draw heavily from pages that already rank. Check how your results look in other countries and languages with the free [Google Search Simulator](/tools/google-search-simulator/), where AI Overviews roll out at different speeds.

Because this area changes monthly, many clients pair it with [SEO consulting](/services/seo-consulting/) so their team understands what is changing and why, rather than reacting to every headline.
