## When you need judgement, not more work

Sometimes the problem is not a lack of hands but a lack of certainty. Your team has a list of forty SEO tasks and no clear order. An agency is proposing a big content push and you do not know whether it is the right bet. A redesign goes live in six weeks and nobody has checked what it will do to your rankings. A traffic drop arrived with a core update and opinions differ on why.

Those are decisions, and decisions are cheaper to get right than to undo.

## How consulting works

Consulting is senior time, prepared in advance. Before each session we look at your Search Console, analytics and the site itself, so the call is spent on the question rather than on background. After each session you get written notes with what was decided and what happens next.

Some engagements start with a single session and turn into a defined project, such as a [technical SEO audit](/services/technical-seo-audit/) when the issues need a full diagnosis, or [domain strategy](/services/domain-strategy/) when a migration or merger is on the table. Others stay as a light monthly retainer: a few hours of review and advice that keeps an in-house team on track.

A recurring topic is how search itself is changing. Many retainers now include a regular review of [AI search optimization](/services/ai-search-optimization/), so leadership understands where AI answers affect their traffic and what to do about it.

If you want to come to the first session prepared, the free [Search Query Builder](/tools/search-query-builder/) is a good start: use `site:` with `inurl:` and `intitle:` to see exactly what Google holds for your domain.

The experience behind the advice is direct: SEO practice since 2009, consulting since 2011, across e-commerce, services and publishing in the EU and US.
