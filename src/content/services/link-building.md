## Why rankings stall without links

You can have the fastest site, the best category structure and the most useful content in your market, and still sit at position eight. Past a certain point, Google needs evidence that other sites trust you. In competitive commercial queries, the sites in the top three almost always have more relevant referring domains than the sites below them.

The opposite mistake is just as common: buying hundreds of cheap links from sites that exist only to sell links. Those rarely help, sometimes harm, and leave you with a profile that has to be cleaned up later.

## How we build links

We start with the gap. A backlink audit compares your referring domains against the pages ranking above you, query by query. That tells us how many links each target page needs and from what kind of site. You can see the raw picture for any domain with the free [Backlink Checker](/tools/backlink-checker/).

Links then go to pages, not to the brand in general. A category sitting at position seven for a valuable query is worth more attention than the home page. Our curated directory and partner network gives us reliable placements on real, indexed sites in the EU and US; outreach and PR campaigns add editorial links on top.

Links work best when there is something worth linking to. Data studies, tools and guides built through [content strategy](/services/content-strategy/) make outreach far easier. For local businesses, citations and city-level links are part of [local SEO](/services/local-seo/).

When a faster path exists, such as redirecting a relevant expired or acquired domain with a clean history, we assess it within [domain strategy](/services/domain-strategy/). Done correctly, consolidating authority from an existing site can be worth years of outreach.
