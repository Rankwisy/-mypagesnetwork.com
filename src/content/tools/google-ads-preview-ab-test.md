## How to use the A/B test tool

Write up to three variations, each with a headline and a description. The counters show how many characters you have left against Google's limits: 30 for a headline and 90 for a description. Then set up to three tests, each a location and a device. The test matrix fills in automatically, with one link per variation and test. Each link opens the live Google results for that headline in that market.

Use the matrix as a checklist. Work through each cell, look at the page, and note what you see in the same spreadsheet where you keep your ad copy.

## What to look for

For each test, look at the ads and listings that appear for your headline.

- **Who else is bidding?** If the top four ads all use the same offer, such as free delivery or a percentage discount, a variation that says the same thing will not stand out.
- **What do the organic results promise?** Shoppers compare ads with the listings under them. If organic results show reviews and prices, ad copy without them looks weaker.
- **Does the market change the picture?** The same headline can face very different competition in the UK and Germany. Variations that win at home may need a different angle abroad.
- **Device differences.** Mobile results show fewer ads above the fold and truncate more aggressively. Switch on your browser's device emulation for the mobile tests.

## How to act on the results
