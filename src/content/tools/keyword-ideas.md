## What the tool returns

Enter one seed keyword and a market. Keyword Ideas returns a list of related queries, each with:

- **Monthly search volume** for the chosen market
- **Keyword difficulty** on a 0 to 100 scale
- **Cost per click**, the average top-of-page bid in Google Ads
- **Search intent**: informational, commercial, transactional or navigational
- **12-month trend**, so you can see seasonality and growth at a glance

Results can be sorted and filtered, so you can quickly find high-volume, low-difficulty queries with buying intent.

## Use cases by role

**E-commerce operators** use it to check whether a category deserves its own page. If "linen bedding" and "linen duvet cover" each have solid volume and transactional intent, they probably need separate, targeted pages rather than one generic "bedding" category.

**SMB owners** use it to choose the services and locations worth a landing page, and to stop writing about topics nobody searches for.

**Content teams** use the informational queries to plan guides that capture research-stage buyers and link down to the pages that sell.

**Paid search managers** use CPC and intent to find queries where organic pages could replace expensive clicks.

## How to act on the results

Group the ideas by intent and by the page they belong to. Each group should map to one page on your site; if no page exists, you have found a gap. A store with hundreds of these groups needs a proper category architecture, which we build in [e-commerce SEO](/services/ecommerce-seo/).

Rising trends deserve attention early. A query that has doubled in the last six months is an opportunity before competitors notice it, which is the basis of our [trend and product research](/services/trend-product-research/). When you have the list, check the live competition for your top targets in the [SERP Checker](/tools/serp-checker/), or explore the query syntax variations yourself with the free [Search Query Builder](/tools/search-query-builder/).

## A five-minute workflow

Start with the term your customers would type, not your internal product name. Run it in your main market, sort by volume, and remove ideas you cannot serve. Then run the same seed in a second market where you sell. Queries with healthy volume, transactional intent and a difficulty below the sites you already outrank are your shortlist. Export it, and note for each query which existing page should rank, or that a new page is needed.
