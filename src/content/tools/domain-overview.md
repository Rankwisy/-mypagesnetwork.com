## What the tool returns

Enter a domain and a market. Domain Overview returns:

- **Estimated monthly organic traffic** from Google in that market
- **Traffic value**, the equivalent cost of that traffic in Google Ads
- **Top ranking keywords**, with position, volume and the URL that ranks
- **A market view**, so you can see where a site is strong and where it is absent

It works on any public domain: your own, a competitor's, or a domain you are thinking about buying.

## Use cases by role

**E-commerce operators** use it to benchmark competitors in each country. If a rival earns three times your organic traffic in Spain from category keywords, you know where to look first.

**SMB owners** use it to understand which competitors actually win search in their market, which is often not the companies they think of as competitors.

**Buyers of websites and domains** use it as a first check before an acquisition: does the site really get the search traffic the seller claims, and which keywords carry it?

**Agencies** use it to size an opportunity before proposing work.

## How to act on the results

Look at the top keywords of the sites beating you. They show which pages and topics drive their traffic, and which of those you do not cover. That gap becomes a content and category plan.

If you are assessing a domain to acquire or redirect, Domain Overview is only the first step. History, link quality and spam signals matter as much as traffic, and redirecting without a URL-level plan loses most of the value. That diligence is what our [domain strategy](/services/domain-strategy/) service covers, often combined with [link building](/services/link-building/) after consolidation.

For a closer look at a domain's link profile, use the [Backlink Checker](/tools/backlink-checker/). To spot technical issues on a site you already own, the free [Search Query Builder](/tools/search-query-builder/) is a quick start.

## A competitor benchmark in four steps

List the three sites that most often rank above you. Run each through Domain Overview in your main market, then in a second market. Record estimated traffic, traffic value and their top ten keywords. Mark the keywords where you have no ranking page at all. Those gaps, sorted by volume, are a ready-made starting point for your next quarter of SEO work.
