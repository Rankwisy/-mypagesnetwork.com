## What the tool returns

Enter a domain or a URL. The Backlink Checker returns:

- **Referring domains**: the unique sites that link to the target
- **Backlinks**: the individual linking pages, with source URL and target URL
- **Anchor text**: the words used in links, and how they are distributed
- **Link types**: follow, nofollow, sponsored, UGC, and image or text links

You can check any public domain, so the same report works for your site and for the competitors ranking above you.

## Use cases by role

**E-commerce operators** use it to understand why a competitor's category page outranks theirs despite weaker content: often the difference is a dozen strong referring domains.

**SMB owners** use it to see which local directories, associations and press sites link to competitors, and which of those they could get listed on too.

**Marketing teams** use it after a campaign to check which links were actually earned and whether they point at the right pages.

**Buyers of domains** use it to check that a domain's links are real, relevant and not built through a link scheme.

## How to act on the results

Compare your referring domains with the top three sites for your target query. Sites that link to two or more of your competitors but not to you are your best prospects. Turning that list into placed links, with directory listings, our partner network and outreach, is what our [link building](/services/link-building/) service does.

Unnatural anchor patterns, such as most links using the exact commercial keyword, suggest a past link scheme. That matters most when buying or redirecting a domain, which is part of [domain strategy](/services/domain-strategy/).

## A link gap check you can run today

Pick one query that matters to you and note the three domains ranking above you. Export their referring domains, then yours. Keep the domains that link to at least two competitors and not to you, and discard any that look like link farms. What remains is a short, realistic prospect list.
