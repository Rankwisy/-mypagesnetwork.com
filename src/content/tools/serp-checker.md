## What the tool returns

Enter a keyword, choose a market and a device. The SERP Checker returns:

- **The top 20 organic results**, with URL, title and position
- **SERP features** present on the page, such as AI Overview, featured snippet, People also ask, local pack, shopping results and video
- **Desktop or mobile view**, since rankings and features often differ between the two

Unlike checking Google from your own browser, the results are not personalised, so you see what a typical searcher in that market sees.

## Use cases by role

**E-commerce operators** use it to see whether Google wants category pages, product pages or guides for a query. If the top ten are all category pages from other stores, a blog post will not rank there.

**SMB owners** use it to check where they stand against local competitors, and whether a local pack or AI Overview is taking the clicks above the organic results.

**Content teams** use it before writing anything: the format, depth and angle of the top results tell you what the brief should be.

**Agencies and consultants** use it to show clients a neutral view of their position, without personalisation.

## How to act on the results

Read the page type first. Match the format Google rewards, then aim to be more useful than what ranks today. Briefs built from this analysis are the core of our [content strategy](/services/content-strategy/) work.

If an AI Overview or featured snippet sits at the top, the goal shifts from ranking to being the source it quotes. That is covered by [AI search optimization](/services/ai-search-optimization/).

Want to see the same page in another country without the data tool? Open it in the free [Google Search Simulator](/tools/google-search-simulator/). And if the pages above you are far stronger, check their links in the [Backlink Checker](/tools/backlink-checker/).

## A quick SERP review checklist

For each target query, note four things: the page type that dominates (category, product, guide, tool), the strongest domains in the top five, the SERP features above position one, and whether mobile differs from desktop. Run the check again a month later. A page type shifting, say from guides to category pages, tells you Google has reread the intent, and your plan should follow before competitors notice.
