## How to use the simulator

Type the query exactly as a customer would. Pick the country you want to check, the interface language and, if you need them, more results per page. The tool shows the Google URL it built, so you can copy it into a report or share it with a colleague. Click "Open in Google" and the results load in a new tab.

Three settings matter:

- **Country** sets Google's `gl` parameter. It changes which country's results and ads you see, which is the main thing you want when checking rankings abroad.
- **Interface language** sets `hl`. It changes the language of Google's own interface and can shift results for multilingual markets such as Belgium, Switzerland or Canada.
- **Results per page** sets `num`, handy when you want to see positions 11 to 50 in one view.

Results are only as clean as your browser. For a neutral view, open the link in a private window while signed out of Google.

## What to look for in the results

Start with your own position for the queries that matter. Then look at who sits above you. In a new market, you are often competing with local retailers and marketplaces you have never heard of, not your usual competitors. Note which result types Google shows: shopping ads, local pack, AI Overview, videos, "People also ask". Each one takes space and clicks from the organic results.

Compare the same query across neighbouring markets. A query where you rank third in the UK and twentieth in Ireland usually points to a hreflang, content or link problem that can be fixed.

## How to act on what you find

If you rank well at home but poorly in another country, check that the right language and country version of your page is being served. Hreflang errors are one of the most common faults we find in a [technical SEO audit](/services/technical-seo-audit/).

If the results are dominated by a local pack, organic rankings alone will not win the clicks. That is where [local SEO](/services/local-seo/) work on your Google Business Profile and citations comes in.

If an AI Overview sits on top, look at which sources it cites. Getting into that answer is a different job from ranking, covered in [AI search optimization](/services/ai-search-optimization/).
